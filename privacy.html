Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Kaku Sync ("the extension", "we") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the extension reads, why, and how you stay in control.

1. What we collect

Only after you grant explicit permission, Kaku Sync reads:

  • Cookies for the domains you have visited
  • Local and session storage from your open tabs
  • Basic browser metadata (user agent, timestamp) for backup integrity

2. How we use it

The data is used solely to create a recoverable backup of your browsing sessions. It is sent only to your configured recovery endpoint. We do not sell, share, or analyse this data.

3. Your control

Nothing is read, stored, or transmitted until you check the consent box. You may revoke permission at any time from the popup. Revocation is immediate.

4. Data retention

Backups live only on your recovery endpoint. You can delete them at any time.

5. Contact

Questions? Open the popup and use the contact link, or reach the maintainer of your installation.
