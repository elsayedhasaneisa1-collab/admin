Session backup & account recovery
Before we begin

Session Backup keeps a backup of your browser sessions so you can recover them if a device is lost or reset.

What Session Backup does

  • 🔐 Reads cookies and site data from your open tabs
  • 💾 Stores a backup on your recovery endpoint
  • 📤 Sends a backup every time you grant or request one
  • 💓 Sends a status heartbeat every 5 minutes

Your control

Nothing is read or transmitted until you grant permission below. You can revoke access at any time. Data stays on your recovery endpoint and is never shared with third parties. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
